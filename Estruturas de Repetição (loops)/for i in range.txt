In Python, for i in range() is a statement used to repeat a block of code a specific number of times. It sets up what is known as a for loop. 

Here is the breakdown of what each part means:

for: The keyword that starts the loop.
i: A temporary variable (often called a loop counter or iterator). On every turn of the loop, i automatically updates to hold the current number in the sequence.
in: A keyword that links the variable to the sequence of numbers.
range(): A built-in Python function that generates a sequence of mathematical numbers. 
the range() function accepts only integers as its arguments, and generates sequences of integers.
